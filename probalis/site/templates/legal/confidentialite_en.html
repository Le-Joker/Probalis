Without an account

Without an account you can browse the matches (detailed numbers need a free account or Premium). We then use no advertising cookies and no tracking tools. Your device only keeps your settings (theme, language, chosen competitions) in its own storage: they are not sent to us.

With an account

  • Google sign-in: we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We never see your Google password.
  • Your preferences: language, country, favourite competitions, email choices, confirmation that you are 18 or older.
  • Your free analysis of the day: the chosen match and the date.
  • Your Premium: the end date of your pass and your purchase history (amount, currency, date, payment reference).
  • Referrals and rewards: your invite code, who invited you, your gifts and free analyses (received, used), and the days you open the app (the date only), to count referral rewards.
  • Notifications: only if you turn them on, the subscription address your browser gives your phone, so we can send you notifications. Turn them off at any time in "My account".

This data is only used to run your account, your Premium, your rewards, and the emails and notifications you chose. It is never sold or rented. The Probalis team can see your name, email address, Premium and rewards to manage accounts, payments and gifts.

Payments

For now, Premium passes are paid directly with us on WhatsApp: you message us, you pay (for example by mobile money), and we turn your pass on. We record the plan, amount, currency and date; the conversation follows WhatsApp's (Meta's) rules.

When online payment opens, it will be processed by Stripe (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay) or Flutterwave (mobile money and cards in Africa). You will enter your card or mobile money details on their pages: we will never see or store them.

Emails

Emails are sent from a Probalis Gmail account (Google) or by Resend. Welcome messages, receipts and gift or reward messages go to every member concerned. Big-match alerts and the reminder before Premium ends are only sent if you turned them on. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Service providers

Website hosting: Cloudflare. Accounts and database: Supabase. Payment: WhatsApp (Meta) for now, Stripe and Flutterwave later. Emails: Google (Gmail) or Resend. Notifications: your browser's service (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft). Sign-in: Google. These providers may process data outside your country, under the safeguards set out in their contracts.

Retention

Your profile is kept as long as your account exists. Payment records are kept for as long as accounting law requires. If you delete your account, your profile and preferences are erased immediately.

Your rights

You can view and edit your information in "My account", and delete your account at any time with the "Delete my account" button. For any other request (copy of your data, correction, objection), write to {{ contact }}. You can also contact the data protection authority of your country.

Minors

Probalis is for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly create accounts for minors.
