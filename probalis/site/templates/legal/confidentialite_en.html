Probalis is published by {{ owner }} (contact: {{ contact }}). This page explains which data we use, why, and how to delete it.

Without an account

You can read every free analysis without an account. We then use no advertising cookies and no tracking tools. Your device only keeps your settings (theme, language, chosen competitions, the match of your free analysis of the day) in its own storage: they are not sent to us.

With an account

  • Google sign-in: we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We never see your Google password.
  • Your preferences: language, country, favourite competitions, email choices, confirmation that you are 18 or older.
  • Your Premium: the end date of your pass and your purchase history (amount, currency, date, payment reference).

This data is only used to run your account, your Premium and the emails you chose. It is never sold or rented.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay) or Flutterwave (mobile money and cards in Africa). You enter your card or mobile money details on their pages: we never see or store them.

Emails

Emails are sent by Resend. Welcome messages and receipts go to every member. Big-match alerts and the reminder before Premium ends are only sent if you turned them on. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Service providers

Website hosting: Cloudflare. Accounts and database: Supabase. Payments: Stripe, Flutterwave. Emails: Resend. Sign-in: Google. These providers may process data outside your country, under the safeguards set out in their contracts.

Retention

Your profile is kept as long as your account exists. Payment records are kept for as long as accounting law requires. If you delete your account, your profile and preferences are erased immediately.

Your rights

You can view and edit your information in "My account", and delete your account at any time with the "Delete my account" button. For any other request (copy of your data, correction, objection), write to {{ contact }}. You can also contact the data protection authority of your country.

Minors

Probalis is for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly create accounts for minors.
