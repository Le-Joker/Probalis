Betting should stay fun. Probalis gives you real probabilities precisely so you know what you're playing: no bet is a sure thing, and nobody beats the bookmakers for certain.

Good habits

  • Set a weekly or monthly budget, and stop once it's spent.
  • Only stake money you can afford to lose, never rent, food or bill money.
  • Never chase your losses.
  • Avoid betting when you're emotional, tired or have been drinking.
  • Take breaks and keep other hobbies.

Warning signs

  • You stake more than planned, or more and more often.
  • You hide your betting from people close to you, or borrow money to bet.
  • You think about betting all the time, or feel bad when you're not betting.

If this sounds like you, talk about it. It's common, and free, confidential help exists.

Protect yourself

  • Most bookmakers offer deposit limits and self-exclusion in the account settings.
  • Free apps block betting sites on your phone, for example BetBlocker.

Get help

  • United Kingdom: National Gambling Helpline, 0808 8020 133, begambleaware.org
  • France: Joueurs Info Service, 09 74 75 13 13, joueurs-info-service.fr
  • Everywhere: Gamblers Anonymous support groups, and your doctor.

Probalis is for people aged 18 or over.
